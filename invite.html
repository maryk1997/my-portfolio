INVITÉ

A digital experience studio built around one observation: an invitation doesn't have to be the last thing people see before an event. It can be the first piece of marketing that makes them want to go.

Role
Founder, Concept, Design, Front-End
Status
Active. Client work and product build, since May 2026
View Code See the projects
THE OBSERVATION

Invitations were treated as an afterthought.

Working on wedding and brand-event sites, I kept seeing the same pattern: the invitation was designed last, shared once and forgotten. A static PDF, or a plain page with a date and a map. Meanwhile the same content, framed as a short digital journey, was clearly capable of doing more: building curiosity before the event, not just confirming logistics after the decision was already made.

That gap between invitation as formality and invitation as marketing became the starting point for INVITÉ.

THE IDEA

One system, shaped differently every time.

INVITÉ is a small studio practice built around a repeatable process of teaser, digital ad, brand reveal, invitation and event, applied to launches, openings and weddings, with each project getting its own art direction rather than a reused template. The five wedding experiences in my work section are the clearest proof of that: same underlying capabilities, five distinct visual identities.

FIRST PROOF POINTS

Built on real client work, not a concept deck.

JETOUR Georgia was the first commercial project run through this approach: a full bilingual event experience with RSVP and live data collection, delivered end to end. A second brand activation, JETOUR / II, followed the same model. Then came Casa Gelso, a luxury fashion house opening in Tbilisi: the first time the full teaser ad reveal invitation sequence ran at campaign scale, in three languages, for a brand's opening night.

Concept & Direction

Defining the narrative arc of an event before a single screen is designed.

Client Communication

Corporate account-management background applied to scoping, expectations and delivery.

Build & QA

Front-end implementation and manual QA under my own ownership, end to end.

WHAT'S NEXT

From service toward product.

The next stage is turning the repeatable parts of this process, the teaser-to-invitation sequence and the RSVP and data-collection layer, into something closer to a lightweight product other event organisers could use directly, rather than a bespoke build each time. That's the direction INVITÉ is heading, alongside the client project work that keeps funding and testing it.
