ABOUT

I didn't start in design.
I started in business.

I spent four years managing large corporate accounts at an insurance group. That taught me how companies actually decide things, how to sit across from a client, and how to carry responsibility for something with money attached to it.

Then I retrained. Front-end development in Barcelona, manual QA after that, and then real client work. I don't experience design, code, testing and business as four separate jobs, because I have never been allowed to do only one of them. I understand the problem, shape the idea, build it, break it on purpose, fix it and ship it.

What I want next is a team. The work above shows I can do it alone, and I would rather do it with people who are better than me at the parts I am still learning, on brands worth the effort, with the stability of a proper job behind it.
